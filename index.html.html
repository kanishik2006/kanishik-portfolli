Who I am

ABOUT ME

I’m Kanishik, a passionate photographer and creative freelancer specializing in photography, thumbnail designing, and visual storytelling.

I work on creative projects, helping individuals, content creators, and businesses present their ideas through attractive visuals.

Along with my freelance work, I also provide photography training classes for beginners who want to learn photography skills, camera handling, and creative techniques.

My goal is to create meaningful visual experiences and help others develop their creative skills.
